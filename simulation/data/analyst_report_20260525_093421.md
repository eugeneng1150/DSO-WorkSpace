| Avg Utility (overall mean) | ~1.87 | ~1.99 | +0.12 |
| False Accusation Rate (mean) | 0.839 | 0.746 | −0.093 |

**Adding reputation to network rewiring worsens both primary outcomes.** The one positive signal is that NR shows slightly lower false accusation rates (0.746 vs. 0.839), suggesting reputation scores provide some informational grounding. However, this does not translate into better cooperation.

**Possible explanations for the negative interaction:**
1. **Reputation scores may trigger excessive link-severing**, fragmenting the trade network and reducing both trading volume and sustainability.
2. **Reputation information may be noisy or manipulable**, leading agents to make worse partner-selection decisions than they would using only their direct experience (as in N alone).
3. **The single NR run may be an outlier** — this is the most parsimonious explanation given n=1.

**Conclusion: Insufficient data to draw reliable conclusions.** The pattern is suggestive of a negative interaction but requires replication.
