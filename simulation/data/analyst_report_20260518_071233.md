## 7) Implications for the research question
**Research question:** Which formal mechanism (or combination) allows self-interested agents to maintain market stability (all four metrics > 0.5)?

### What the data supports
- **No mechanism achieves full stability** under the strict criterion.
- **Reputation (R)** and **Contracting (C)** tend to improve **Sustainability** and often **Peace**.
- **RM** appears most promising for **Peace + Sustainability + Equality** and dramatically reduces **false accusations** (final_mean **0.0**).

### What the data does *not* support
- **Efficiency improvement to >0.5** is not achieved in any condition:
  - Best observed final_mean Efficiency is **RM: 0.117** (still far below 0.5).
- Therefore, the simulation results do not identify a mechanism that enables **both cooperation and high realized trade gains**.

### Likely interpretation
- Mechanisms may reduce defection (raising Peace), but **do not sufficiently increase realized trade opportunities/terms** (Efficiency stays near zero).
- Alternatively, the **Efficiency metric may be computed in a way that is insensitive to the mechanism changes** (or the mechanism affects defection but not matching/production decisions).

### Data insufficiency / next steps
To answer the research question conclusively, you would need:
- More runs (to detect rare stable trajectories) and/or
- Mechanism variants that directly target **trade realization** (not just defection/accusations), and/or
- Verification that **warning_accuracy** is functioning (currently always 0.0).
