=== YOUR ACTIONS THIS ROUND ===

You may take any combination of the following actions. Output a JSON array.
Each element is an object with an "action" field plus the required parameters.

Available actions (always):
  {"action": "produce",       "good": "A"|"B"|"C",  "quantity": int (0-5)}
  {"action": "send_private",  "target": "{agent_id}", "text": "..."}
  {"action": "send_public",   "text": "..."}
  {"action": "propose_trade", "target": "{agent_id}", "offer": {"good": "X", "quantity": int}, "request": {"good": "Y", "quantity": int}}
  {"action": "accept_trade",  "trade_id": "..."}
  {"action": "reject_trade",  "trade_id": "..."}
  {"action": "defect_trade",  "trade_id": "..."}

Available actions (mechanism-dependent — see mechanism block above):
  {"action": "propose_contract",        "target": "{agent_id}", "terms": {...}}   [+Contracting only]
  {"action": "sign_contract",           "contract_id": "..."}                      [+Contracting only]
  {"action": "reject_contract",         "contract_id": "..."}                      [+Contracting only]
  {"action": "delegate_to_mediator",    "trade_id": "..."}                         [+Mediation only]

Your goal is to maximise your total utility over all rounds.
Think carefully about the long-run consequences of defection — a short-term gain may cost you future trade partners.

Output your chosen actions as a JSON array:
[
  { "action": "...", ... },
  { "action": "...", ... }
