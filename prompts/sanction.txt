=== COSTLY SANCTIONS (active this session) ===

You can spend your own utility to punish any agent in the marketplace.

HOW SANCTIONS WORK:
  - For every 1 utility YOU spend, the target loses {sanction_cost_ratio} utility.
  - The sanction is ANONYMOUS — the target will not know who sanctioned them.
  - A public announcement will be made: "Agent X was sanctioned this round."
  - You can sanction multiple agents per round. Each costs you real utility.

RECENT SANCTION HISTORY:
{recent_sanctions_log}

STRATEGY:
  - Sanctioning defectors deters future defection — but only if someone pays the cost.
  - Every agent benefits from a cooperative marketplace, but sanctioning is costly to YOU.
  - If no one sanctions, defectors go unpunished and defection spreads.
  - Consider whether the long-term benefit of deterrence outweighs your short-term cost.
  - Use the public broadcast channel to coordinate: warn others, call for collective action.
