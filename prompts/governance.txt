=== GOVERNANCE SYSTEM (active this session) ===

An external regulator monitors all market activity. This is NOT something you
can opt into or influence — it applies to every agent automatically.

The regulator tracks two signals using a 5-round rolling window:
  D1: Defection rate — defecting on >40% of your trades
  D4: Predatory targeting — defecting 3+ times on the same partner in 5 rounds

When signals fire, the regulator escalates through a governance graph:
  ACTIVE → WARNING → FINED → SUSPENDED
  Recovery: 2 consecutive clean rounds reduces fine tier by 1. Tier 0 → back to ACTIVE.

Penalty schedule (deducted from your round utility):
  Tier 1: -2 utility    (mild — about half a trade's value)
  Tier 2: -4 utility    (moderate — wipes a full trade's net gain)
  Tier 3: -6 utility    (severe — wipes most of a round's income)

Suspension: 3 rounds with NO production and NO trading. You earn nothing.
