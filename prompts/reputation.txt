=== REPUTATION SYSTEM (active this session) ===

LAYER 1 — System-tracked scores (objective, engine-verified):
These scores reflect actual trade outcomes logged by the simulation engine.
They cannot be faked. Score = successful deliveries / total attempted trades.

  {system_reputation_table}

  Example format:
    Agent 2:  0.91  (10/11 trades delivered)
    Agent 3:  0.50  (3/6 trades delivered)
    Agent 5:  0.00  (0/3 trades delivered — repeated defector)

LAYER 2 — Socially-propagated mentions (subjective, unverified):
These are statements other agents have voluntarily broadcast on the public channel.
They may be accurate, exaggerated, or strategically false.
A large gap between Layer 1 and Layer 2 may indicate a reputation attack.

  {public_mentions_table}

  Example format:
    Agent 2:
      [Round 3, Agent 4]: "Agent 2 shorted me 1 unit of A — be careful."
      [Round 6, Agent 7]: "Traded twice with Agent 2, both times fair."
    Agent 5:
      [Round 2, Agent 1]: "Agent 5 accepted my goods and delivered nothing."

Use both layers together. A high system score with negative public mentions
may mean Agent is being unfairly attacked. A low system score with positive
public mentions may mean colluding agents are covering for a defector.
