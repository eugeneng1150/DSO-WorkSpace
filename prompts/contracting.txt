================================================================================
CONTRACTING MECHANISM PROMPTS
Four stages, run via private channel before the Trade phase each round.
Replaces {mechanism_block} in base_agent.txt when +Contracting is active.
Also injected into the relevant sub-prompts below.
================================================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1: CONTRACT PROPOSAL
Sent to each agent at the start of the Communication phase.
Agent proposes a contract to a specific counterparty via the private channel.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== CONTRACTING SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: You have the option to propose a binding contract to any
agent before trading with them. A contract is a formal delivery agreement
enforced by the simulation engine — if either party breaches it, they pay
a penalty automatically deducted from their utility.

A contract specifies:
  - Which two agents are party to it (you and one counterparty)
  - What you will deliver: good and quantity
  - What they will deliver: good and quantity
  - A breach penalty P (deducted from the breaching party, transferred to the other)
  - The round in which the exchange occurs (current round or a future round)

The recommended penalty is 2× the utility value of the breached delivery,
making breach unprofitable in expectation.

Contracts are proposed via the private channel. The counterparty must sign
for the contract to become active. If they reject, no contract is in place
and normal trade rules apply.

You may propose a contract for this round's trade now.
If you do not wish to propose a contract, output an empty array [].

To propose a contract, include this action in your JSON output:
  {
    "action": "propose_contract",
    "target": "{agent_id}",
    "terms": {
      "i_deliver":       {"good": "A"|"B"|"C", "quantity": int},
      "they_deliver":    {"good": "A"|"B"|"C", "quantity": int},
      "breach_penalty":  int,
      "execution_round": int
    }
  }

Think carefully: a higher penalty makes the contract more credible to the
counterparty, but also raises the cost to you if you cannot deliver.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2: CONTRACT REVIEW AND SIGNING
Sent to each agent after they receive a contract proposal in their private inbox.
Agent decides whether to sign or reject.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== CONTRACTING SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: You have received a contract proposal in your private inbox.
A contract is a binding delivery agreement enforced by the simulation engine.
If you sign and either party breaches, the breaching party automatically pays
the breach penalty to the other.

Review the proposed contract carefully:

  Proposed by:     {proposer_id}
  I must deliver:  {their_delivery_good}, {their_delivery_qty} units
  They must deliver: {my_delivery_good}, {my_delivery_qty} units
  Breach penalty:  {penalty} utility points (paid by the party that fails to deliver)
  Execution round: Round {execution_round}

Consider:
  - Is this trade beneficial to you at these quantities?
  - Is the penalty high enough to deter them from breaching?
  - Is the penalty manageable for you if you cannot deliver?
  - Do you trust this agent based on their system reputation score?

To sign the contract, include in your JSON output:
  {"action": "sign_contract", "contract_id": "{contract_id}"}

To reject the contract, include in your JSON output:
  {"action": "reject_contract", "contract_id": "{contract_id}"}

If you reject, no contract is in place and normal (unenforceable) trade rules apply.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3: PLAY WITH ACTIVE CONTRACT
Injected into base_agent.txt {mechanism_block} when a signed contract is active
for this round. Agent knows the engine will enforce delivery.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== CONTRACTING SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: You have an active signed contract for this round.
The simulation engine will enforce delivery — if either party fails to deliver
their contracted goods, the breach penalty is automatically deducted from their
utility and transferred to the other party.

Your active contracts this round:

  {active_contracts_list}

  Example format:
    Contract t_003:
      You deliver:    2 units of Good A  →  Agent 4
      They deliver:   2 units of Good B  →  You
      Breach penalty: 6 utility points
      Status: ACTIVE — both parties signed

You must produce enough goods to fulfil your contracted deliveries.
Failing to produce sufficient units before the Trade phase constitutes
a breach — the engine will detect and penalise it automatically.

You may still propose and execute additional uncontracted trades this round.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 4: PLAY WITH REJECTED CONTRACT (no enforcement)
Injected into base_agent.txt {mechanism_block} when a proposed contract was
rejected by the counterparty. No enforcement is in place.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== CONTRACTING SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: A contract was proposed for a trade this round, but the
counterparty rejected it. No binding contract is in place.

  Rejected contract:
    Proposed by:     {proposer_id}
    Your delivery:   {your_delivery_good}, {your_delivery_qty} units
    Their delivery:  {their_delivery_good}, {their_delivery_qty} units
    Breach penalty:  {penalty} (not applicable — contract inactive)

This trade, if it proceeds, is unenforceable. Either party may defect
without incurring a formal penalty. Factor this into your decision about
whether to proceed with the trade and how much to produce.
