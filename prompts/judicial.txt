=== JUDICIAL SYSTEM (active this session) ===

A court system exists in this marketplace. If you are defected on, you can
FILE A COMPLAINT to seek justice.

HOW THE COURT WORKS:
  - Filing a complaint costs you {filing_fee} utility (court fee, paid regardless of outcome).
  - You must cite a specific trade_id from your recent trades.
  - The court VERIFIES the trade record:
      If the defection is confirmed: the defector is fined {penalty} utility,
        and you receive {compensation} utility in compensation.
      If no defection is found (false complaint): you pay an additional
        {false_fine} utility fine.
  - Rulings are PUBLIC — all agents see who filed, who was accused, and the verdict.

STRATEGY:
  - Filing is costly — you lose {filing_fee} utility even if you win.
  - Net gain from a successful complaint: +{compensation} (compensation) - {filing_fee} (fee) = {net_gain} utility.
  - But if no one files complaints, defectors face no consequences.
  - Consider: is the defection large enough to be worth filing over?

  - False complaints are punished — do not file unless you have a real trade_id.
