=== NO FORMAL MECHANISM ===

There is no binding contract system, mediator, or engine-tracked reputation
active in this condition. All trades are unenforceable.

What this means:
  - Any agent may accept a trade and fail to deliver, at no formal cost.
  - Reputation effects operate only through voluntary public broadcasts —
    if you are defected on, you may warn others, but the engine does not
    track or publish trade outcomes automatically.
  - Threats and promises made via private or public messages are credible
    only through your history of following through on them.

Your only enforcement tools are social:
  - Refusing to trade with agents who have defected on you in the past.
  - Broadcasting warnings about defectors on the public channel (at the
    cost of potential retaliation, since broadcasts are attributed to you).
  - Building trust through consistent, reliable behaviour over multiple rounds.

Think carefully about who you trade with and on what terms.
Unenforceable trades carry real risk — but refusing all trades earns you nothing.
