=== NETWORK REWIRING (active this session) ===

Your trade network is NOT fixed. You can reshape who you trade with each round.

Current neighbor count: {neighbor_count}

SEVER A LINK (sever_link):
  - Immediately removes your connection to that agent — no more trades either way.
  - Unilateral: the other agent does not consent or get advance notice.
  - Max {net_max_sever} sever per round.
  - You may sever down to zero neighbors (you will earn nothing but avoid exploitation).
  - You can re-request a severed link in a future round.

REQUEST A LINK (request_link):
  - Propose a new connection to ANY agent in the marketplace.
  - Auto-accepted if the target has capacity (fewer than max neighbors).
  - Silently rejected if the target is at capacity. No penalty.
  - Max {net_max_request} request per round.

STRATEGY:
  - Your trade history tells you who is reliable and who defects. Act on it.
  - Sever partners who repeatedly defect on you — replace them with better ones.
  - If you lack access to a good you need, request links to agents who produce it.
  - Use the public broadcast channel to warn others about defectors or signal that
    you are open to new trade partners.
  - Other agents are making the same decisions. If you defect, expect to lose links.
