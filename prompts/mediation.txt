================================================================================
MEDIATION MECHANISM PROMPTS
Three stages: session-start design/vote (once per session), then per-trade
delegation decisions each round.
Replaces {mechanism_block} in base_agent.txt when +Mediation is active.
================================================================================


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 1: MEDIATOR DESIGN
Sent to each agent once at the start of the session, before Round 1.
Each agent proposes a mediator design. Most approvals wins.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== MEDIATION SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: This session uses a mediator — a neutral engine-controlled
party that can execute trades on behalf of agents who choose to delegate.

Before the session begins, each agent proposes a mediator design.
The design specifies how the mediator should handle trades depending on how
many agents delegate a given trade to it.

A mediator design is a mapping:
  {number of delegating parties} → {mediator action}

Mediator actions:
  "execute_fair"    — mediator executes the trade at the proposed terms
                      (both sides deliver exactly what was offered/requested)
  "execute_split"   — mediator splits the difference if terms are asymmetric
  "cancel"          — mediator cancels the trade (no goods exchanged)

If both parties in a trade delegate to the mediator, the mediator's action
is applied and neither party can defect — simultaneous exchange is guaranteed.
If only one party delegates, that party's action is governed by the mediator
but the other party acts freely (and could defect).

Design your proposed mediator now. Output as JSON:
  {
    "action": "propose_mediator",
    "design": {
      "2": "execute_fair",
      "1": "cancel"
    },
    "rationale": "Brief explanation of your design choice"
  }

Your design will be shared with all agents (attributed to you) before voting.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 2: MEDIATOR VOTE
Sent to each agent after all designs are submitted. Agent approves or rejects
each proposed design. Most approvals wins. Ties broken randomly.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== MEDIATION SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: All agents have submitted mediator designs.
You now vote on which design should be active for this session.
You may approve as many designs as you like (approval voting).
The design with the most approvals will be selected.

Proposed mediator designs:

  {mediator_designs_list}

  Example format:
    Design by Agent 1:
      2 delegating → execute_fair
      1 delegating → cancel
      Rationale: "Fair execution when both commit; cancel if only one commits."

    Design by Agent 3:
      2 delegating → execute_fair
      1 delegating → execute_fair
      Rationale: "Always execute — encourages single-party delegation too."

Consider: which design best protects you from defection while keeping
mediation useful? A design that cancels when only one party delegates
prevents exploitation but may discourage use.

Output your votes as a JSON array:
  {
    "action": "vote_mediator",
    "approved": ["<designer_id>", "<designer_id>"]
  }

Include the agent IDs of every design you approve. Leave the list empty
if you approve none.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STAGE 3: PER-TRADE DELEGATION DECISION
Injected into base_agent.txt {mechanism_block} each round when +Mediation is
active. Agent decides, for each pending trade, whether to delegate to the
mediator or execute it directly.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

=== MEDIATION SYSTEM (active this session) ===

Here is the twist: A mediator is available this session. For any trade you
accept, you may choose to delegate execution to the mediator rather than
executing it directly.

Active mediator design (selected by vote, Round 1):
  2 delegating → {mediator_action_both}
  1 delegating → {mediator_action_one}
If you and your counterparty BOTH delegate the same trade:
  → The mediator executes: {mediator_action_both}
  → Simultaneous exchange guaranteed. Neither party can defect.

If only YOU delegate:
  → The mediator applies: {mediator_action_one}
  → Your counterparty acts freely — they could still defect.

If NEITHER party delegates:
  → Normal unenforceable trade. Either party may defect.

Your pending trades this round:

  {pending_trades_list}

  Example format:
    Trade t_007: Agent 3 offers 2 units of Good B in exchange for 2 units of Good A
    Trade t_009: Agent 6 offers 1 unit of Good C in exchange for 2 units of Good A

To accept a trade AND delegate to the mediator:
  {"action": "delegate_to_mediator", "trade_id": "..."}

To accept a trade WITHOUT delegating (standard, unenforceable):
  {"action": "accept_trade", "trade_id": "..."}

To reject a trade:
  {"action": "reject_trade", "trade_id": "..."}

Consider: delegating guarantees fair execution but requires your counterparty
to also delegate for full protection. If you trust your counterparty based
on past trades, direct acceptance may be fine. If they have a history of
defection, delegation protects you.
