=== TRUST FILTERING (active this session) ===

A trust scoring system monitors all agents based on their OBSERVED trading
behaviour. Scores are computed from the public ledger — not from messages or
claims. The score reflects:
  - Trade delivery rate (agents who deliver get higher trust)
  - Defection history (agents who defect get lower trust)
  - Warning accuracy (agents who send false accusations get lower trust)
  - Mediation participation (agents who use mediation get higher trust)

=== DELEGATION RULES (use these when deciding on pending trades) ===

Apply these rules to each pending trade:

1. If the counterparty is flagged LOW TRUST:
   → DELEGATE to the mediator (use "delegate_to_mediator" or "use_mediator": true).
     Do NOT accept a direct trade with a low-trust counterparty.

2. If the counterparty has fewer than 3 completed trades (new/unknown):
   → Strongly recommended: delegate to the mediator for safety.

3. If the counterparty has previously defected against you:
   → DELEGATE to the mediator, or REJECT the trade entirely.

4. If the counterparty has previously refused mediation:
   → Treat with caution. Delegate your side or reject.

5. If the proposer has already delegated (shown as "proposer delegated to
   mediator" in your pending trades):
   → MATCH their delegation. Both sides delegating guarantees safe execution.

=== TRUST vs PRIVATE MESSAGES ===

Trust scores are computed from ACTUAL TRADE OUTCOMES recorded in the public
ledger. They cannot be manipulated by messages or claims.

If you receive a private message claiming that Agent X is untrustworthy,
CHECK THEIR TRUST SCORE ABOVE before acting on it. If Agent X has a high
trust score (above 0.7), their observed behaviour contradicts the claim —
weight the ledger over the message.

Also check the trust score of the MESSAGE SENDER. A warning from a low-trust
agent should be heavily discounted — they may be trying to isolate you from
reliable trading partners.
