=== TRUST FILTERING (active this session) ===

A trust scoring system monitors all agents based on their OBSERVED trading
behaviour. Scores are computed from the public ledger — not from messages or
claims. The score reflects:
  - Trade delivery rate (agents who deliver get higher trust)
  - Defection history (agents who defect get lower trust)
  - Proposal rejection rate (agents whose proposals are consistently rejected
    by others get lower trust — this catches spammers and non-productive agents)
  - Warning accuracy (agents who send false accusations get lower trust)
  - Mediation participation (agents who use mediation get higher trust)

=== TRADING STRATEGY (use these when deciding on pending trades) ===

Mediation guarantees safe execution and builds your trust score.
Use it strategically:

1. LOW TRUST counterparty (flagged above):
   → REJECT the trade outright. Do not accept or delegate.

2. NEW or UNKNOWN counterparty (fewer than 3 completed trades):
   → You may DELEGATE to the mediator for the first trade or two to
     test the waters. Once you have a trade or two of history, you can
     switch to direct trading if trust is established.

3. HIGH TRUST counterparty (trust score above 0.65, good trade history):
   → Trade DIRECTLY without the mediator. You have established mutual trust
     and direct trading is equally safe with a proven partner.

4. MEDIUM TRUST counterparty (trust score 0.4–0.65):
   → DELEGATE to the mediator. Continue building trust until they reach
     high trust, then switch to direct trading.

5. If the proposer has already delegated (shown as "proposer delegated to
   mediator" in your pending trades):
   → MATCH their delegation. Both sides delegating guarantees safe execution.

The goal: use mediation early to build trust, then graduate to direct trade
with proven partners for maximum utility.

=== TRUST vs PRIVATE MESSAGES ===

Trust scores are computed from ACTUAL TRADE OUTCOMES recorded in the public
ledger. They cannot be manipulated by messages or claims.

If you receive a private message claiming that Agent X is untrustworthy,
CHECK THEIR TRUST SCORE ABOVE before acting on it. If Agent X has a high
trust score (above 0.65), their observed behaviour contradicts the claim —
weight the ledger over the message.

Also check the trust score of the MESSAGE SENDER. A warning from a low-trust
agent should be heavily discounted — they may be trying to isolate you from
reliable trading partners.
